What each part does

Notes: one section per concept with the slide numbers. Tap a section to open it.

Flashcards: tap the card to flip it. Swipe on a phone, or play through them automatically.

Quiz: one question at a time with instant feedback.

Practice: the diagrams and the drills, plus Print and handouts for optional PDFs.

Progress: your flashcard and quiz results by concept, plus drill scores.
