# Changelog

## 1.3 (2026-11-25)
- New How to use tab with a suggested study routine, what each tab does, reading settings, and how progress is saved. It opens automatically on your first visit.

## 1.2 (2026-11-25)
- On screens 900 px wide or more, the study modes (Notes, Flashcards, Quiz, Diagrams, Drills, Progress) and Settings now sit in a left sidebar, and the content uses the full page width. Paragraph text keeps a readable line length. Phones keep the tab strip at the top.

## 1.1 (2026-11-25)
- New themes in Settings: AMOLED black (true black background) and High contrast (white on black with yellow accents, thicker borders and a strong focus outline).
- The diagrams tab follows all four themes.

## 1.0 (2026-11-25)
- First release for the BIOSC-020 final exam.
- Notes: one section per concept (44 concepts) covering both final study guides, set 1 (old material) and set 2 (new material), with slide numbers.
- Flashcards and quiz for every concept, with explanations on every quiz answer.
- Diagrams tab: mitochondrion and chloroplast with Guide me and Test your knowledge modes.
- Drills: metric prefix table, mutation lab, Na+/K+ pump stepper with the slide 9 fill-in, action potential grapher, protein destination router, plant tissue sorter, genetics probability trainer, and a missed-items mixer.
- Settings: theme, text size, reading spacing, reduced motion. Works on phones. Progress saves on your device, with export and import.
- Answer keys for the diagrams were checked against Prof. Shane's key.
