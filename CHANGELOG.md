# Changelog

## 1.4 (2026-11-25)
- The notes page now has a short list of ways to study besides reading: put explanations in your own words, rebuild tables like puzzles, use Read aloud, and draw pictures.
- New Table puzzles section in Print and handouts. Each deck has a blank mat and cards to cut out and rearrange, plus an answer key. Decks: metric prefix table, mutation table, genetics tables, endomembrane sorter, and plant sorters.
- On desktop, the Done with this step banner has a small X to hide it until the next step.
- On phones, the slim bar at the top now has a Next button for the following step, so you do not have to scroll to the bottom.
- On desktop, the "Done with this step?" button now sits in a small banner at the top right that stays in view as you scroll, instead of at the very bottom of the page. The optional print link is in the Print and handouts tab.
- On phones, only a slim bar with the current step and a Menu button stays at the top while you scroll, instead of the whole step menu.
- The step banner now lines up with the page content, and the active menu item uses its step color.
- Set 1 and Set 2 labels use one slate color, filled for Set 1 and outlined for Set 2. The leftover orange is gone.
- Quiz feedback skips the explanation when it only repeats the answer.
- Progress now groups concepts by set and shows a small bar for cards and for the quiz.
- Practice cards have a colored top edge and an arrow. Flashcard buttons line up. Small flashcard labels are larger.
- Diagram page: buttons use the site's blue, blanks have stronger outlines and fills, labels have a background so lines do not cross them, and the panels fit their content.
- On phones the diagram can be swiped sideways at a readable size, and Guide me scrolls to the step.
- Removed a stray Show menu button that appeared at the bottom of the page on phones.
- Read aloud buttons on Notes, Flashcards and Quiz, with a speed setting. They use your device's built-in voice.
- Guided mode: pick one concept and go through the notes, flashcards and quiz one step at a time, with a single Next button.
- New Low-stim mode theme with calm colors, no gradient or illustration, and no motion.
- Quiz feedback is calmer: wrong answers no longer use red, and the Settings now has an option to hide quiz scores.
- Instructions on the How to use page, the step banners and the Print page are reworded in shorter, plainer sentences.
- Handout bars now have the Download and Answer key buttons at the left edge, full height, with a visible label under each icon, and the color stripe on the right.
- The handout buttons are now icons, an arrow for the download and a key for the answer key, with a hover description on each.
- Ready to download is now a list of bars, each with a Download button, an Answer key button and the title, in the teal to purple colors.
- On phones, the note about choosing concepts now has its own Choose concepts button right in it, because the main button scrolls out of view.
- New look for the How to use page: a header with a diagram illustration, concept, quiz and flashcard counts, and a route of five cards, one per study step.
- The five study steps now use a teal to purple color scheme in the menu, the step banners and the route cards, in every theme.
- Reporting a mistake now points to a Canvas message, with a show-my-name and show-my-email button, instead of GitHub.
- New Print and handouts page: build a practice test with an answer key from your chosen concepts, print flashcard sheets, and download blank diagrams and premade tests as PDFs.
- Diagrams and Drills are now one step called Practice, so the routine has five steps.
- Missed-items mixer now explains what it is, which modes it pulls from, how chosen concepts limit it, and how your answers change what comes up.
- Choose concepts is now available on every page (except Diagrams) and the explanatory note is gone.
- On phones, all the study steps are now shown as a grid of buttons at the top instead of a strip you had to swipe sideways.
- The sidebar now shows the study routine as numbered steps: 1 Notes, 2 Flashcards, 3 Quiz, 4 Diagrams or Drills, 5 Progress, each with a short description.
- Each page has a Step banner at the top saying what to do, a reminder to choose more or fewer concepts where that matters, and a Next step button at the bottom.
- Metric prefix table: smaller text boxes and narrower columns.
- Mutation lab: the template DNA is color coded by base (A, C, G, T and U each have their own color), and the codon table is replaced by a codon wheel in the style of slide 24.
- Na+/K+ pump drill: the slide 9 heading moved into its frame so the two frames line up.
- Protein destination router: instructions rewritten as five numbered steps.
- Sidebar order is now How to use, Choose concepts, then Notes, Flashcards and the rest. How to use is the page the tool opens on.
- When Choose concepts is dimmed, a short note under it says to select a mode below to choose concepts.
- Mutation lab: every amino acid has its own color in the answers, the explanations and the genetic code table, so silent changes keep a color and missense changes switch it.
- Na+/K+ pump drill: smaller stepper that stays on screen beside the slide 9 paragraph on wide screens, and centered above it on narrow ones.
- On wide screens every page except Diagrams now uses a centered, narrower column instead of stretching across the whole window.
- Match-the-answer tables in the drills (plant tissues, endomembrane, genetics terms) are narrower and centered.
- Sidebar top row now has small icon buttons: hide menu, screen mode (pick a theme) and home (back to How to use).
- Removed the intro text above the diagrams so they fit on screen without scrolling.
- How to use page is more colorful: colored titles and side bars, black body text, and plain text links such as Open Notes at the end of each step.
- Hide menu button at the top of the sidebar (Show menu brings it back), remembered between visits. The sidebar collapses by itself on the Diagrams tab so the diagrams are larger.
- Choose concepts now sits at the top of the left sidebar, above the study modes. It is dimmed on tabs that do not use it.
- Changelog button now sits above Settings in the sidebar and opens the changelog and reporting info in a pop-up. Phones keep it at the bottom of the page.
- Flashcards can now play through on their own. Set the seconds on the question and on the answer (1 to 120), choose whether to start over at the end, and press Play. Tap the card or use Previous and Next to take over, and it pauses when you switch tabs or leave the page.

## 1.3 (2026-11-25)

## 1.2 (2026-11-25)
- On screens 900 px wide or more, the study modes (Notes, Flashcards, Quiz, Diagrams, Drills, Progress) and Settings now sit in a left sidebar, and the content uses the full page width. Paragraph text keeps a readable line length. Phones keep the tab strip at the top.

## 1.1 (2026-11-25)
- New themes in Settings: AMOLED black (true black background) and High contrast (white on black with yellow accents, thicker borders and a strong focus outline).
- The diagrams tab follows all four themes.

## 1.0 (2026-11-25)
- First release for the BIOSC-020 final exam.
- Notes: one section per concept (44 concepts) covering both final study guides, set 1 (old material) and set 2 (new material), with slide numbers.
- Flashcards and quiz for every concept, with explanations on every quiz answer.
- Diagrams tab: mitochondrion and chloroplast with Guide me and Test your knowledge modes.
- Drills: metric prefix table, mutation lab, Na+/K+ pump stepper with the slide 9 fill-in, action potential grapher, protein destination router, plant tissue sorter, genetics probability trainer, and a missed-items mixer.
- Settings: theme, text size, reading spacing, reduced motion. Works on phones. Progress saves on your device, with export and import.
- Answer keys for the diagrams were checked against Prof. Shane's key.
