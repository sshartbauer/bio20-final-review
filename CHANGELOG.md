# Changelog

## 1.5 (2026-11-26)
- A small red dot next to the subtitle in the top bar, as a test that the nightly publish worked. It will be removed.
- Read aloud now pronounces intron and exon correctly.
- Read aloud now says a plus sign before a number as positive, like negative 100 to positive 50 millivolts.
- Read aloud now says science text more clearly: acronyms are spelled out (ETC, RMP, NADPH, tRNA and more), ions and formulas are spoken (sodium, H 2 O, bicarbonate), genotypes (AA, Aa, aa) and crosses, fractions, arrows, plus and minus signs, units (millivolts, grams), Roman numerals (complex 2, photosystem 1), vs, and DNA sequences read in groups of three letters.
- Read aloud now spells out ER, so rough ER is no longer read as rougher.
- Choose concepts buttons now appear everywhere your concepts are used: in the Notes, Flashcards, Quiz and Practice step notes, in the floating Done with this step box, in the Practice explainer, and when no concepts, cards or questions match.
- Read aloud now says exponents and charges out loud: 10 to the power of negative 9, K plus, Ca 2 plus.
- On phones, the How to use button now has a home icon beside it, so it is clear that it takes you back to the start.
- The installed app's fixed bar color (the bottom bar on some phones) is now dark gray-blue instead of bright blue.
- The phone status bar and bottom bar now match your color theme and screen mode instead of staying blue.
- Install directions for computers now mention that Chrome may word the menu item "Install and create shortcut" and put it near the bottom of the three-dot menu.
- New app icon, made from the cell and DNA picture. The Print and handouts page now has directions for installing this site as an app on Android, iPhone, and a computer.
- On the How to use page, the line about the small icons is now a list with a picture of each icon.
- The How to use page titles are now colored bars that touch the top and sides of their frames. The color themes are explained in Make it easier to read.
- Every color theme now works in every screen mode: Follow device, Light, Dark, AMOLED black, and Low-stim. Pick a theme and a mode in the color pop-up. High contrast looks the same for every theme.
- Theme names in the color pop-up are centered under their pictures.
- The color themes now open in a pop-up window with a picture of each theme. Pick one and it changes right away.
- New color theme: Shane's Ducati 996, black and charcoal with silver, a little gold, and one touch of red.
- The color wheel button is now on the phone and tablet top bar, next to Settings.
- New: a color wheel button next to the home button opens a pop-up window of color themes, including Kim's teddy bear, Tommy's car upholstery, and Omar's soccer jersey. There are thirteen to try, including a plain black, white and grey one.
- Removed the font and style note from the top of the Print page.
- The Anki section has a new Download and install menu with links for computer, Android, and iPhone.
- More space between the sections on the Print page.
- The Anki explainer is shorter. The steps and the guide and video links are now in two open-and-close sections.
- Section title bars on the Print page now touch the top and sides of their frames.
- Build a practice test is now two steps. Click Choose concepts, pick your concepts in a window, then download the test and answer key. It no longer uses the concepts chosen on the left.

- Print and handouts has Anki decks (.apkg files) for all cards, each set, and each topic. The Flashcards page links to them.
- The contact note now says you can also share your experience using the site.
- The Flashcards page has a link to the printable flashcard decks.

- The notes page now has a short list of ways to study besides reading: put explanations in your own words, rebuild tables like puzzles, use Read aloud, and draw pictures.
- New Table puzzles section in Print and handouts. Each deck has a blank mat and cards to cut out and rearrange, plus an answer key. Decks: metric prefix table, mutation table, genetics tables, endomembrane sorter, and plant sorters.
